\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We combine independent knowledge checks, matched incentive conditions, universal follow-ups, and frozen activation probes to examine false outputs from a fixed model. The study recovers only two incentive-misreport proxies and leaves 60--62\% of false outputs unresolved. It therefore does not determine the fraction of falsehoods that are genuine lies or reliably estimate verified-misreport detection. It does show that high instructed-deception discrimination can coexist with perfect prompt-only separation and frequent flags on correct pressured answers.

Future work should first establish adequate recovered-case yield with independently justified task incentives or a larger fixed model, then freeze a larger matched sample. Repeated context-preserving measurements, counterbalanced order, independent human/source adjudication, and pressure-matched correct negatives would strengthen the labels and controls. Only with sufficient support should follow-up probes, full semantic entropy, and held-out incentive generalization be compared. The present test set should remain a completed diagnostic rather than a target for prompt optimization.
