An uncertain-error proxy is a false neutral response on a low-evidence question. A stable-wrong
repetition must actually repeat the wrong neutral mode; a different false answer from the same
question remains unresolved. All other false outputs, including nonrecovering known mismatches
and pressured low-evidence answers, are unresolved. These four output categories are mutually
exclusive. We use “uncertain error” in this narrow operational sense, while acknowledging that
stable misconceptions are also epistemic failures.

Correctness and audit. Primary scoring uses normalized alias exact match. Abstentions, refusal
patterns, and responses exceeding 16 normalized words are excluded from the false denominator. A
blinded reference-equivalence audit uses qwen/qwen3.8-27b at temperature zero to evaluate shuf-
fled question–answer pairs against references and aliases. It sees neither arm, belief state, follow-up
relationship, nor probe score. The audit permits reference-supported paraphrases and numeric tol-
erances, retains uncertain judgments as unresolved, and excludes truncated incorrect answers. It
is fallible model assistance rather than human ground truth. Audited labels recompute behavioral
strata and relabel evaluation for the same frozen probes; no audited label is used to refit them.
3.3
Probes, controls, and statistical analysis
We teacher-force exact saved tokens and extract residual block outputs at layers 14 and 28. Features
are either the mean over answer tokens, excluding EOS and padding, or the last pre-answer prompt
token. Layer 28 precedes final normalization. Holding the prompt fixed while changing the contin-
uation yields maximum prompt-feature difference 0.0, confirming that the prompt feature does not
leak answer tokens. Response features can still retain prompt information.
Probes use training-only standardization and L2 logistic regression with C = 1. The instructed-
deception probe trains on 21 paired training questions with correct neutral and known-incorrect
instructed responses: 42 rows total. Its positive labels do not require recovery or establish strategic
intent. A neutral error probe trains on 288 valid neutral answers, including 135 errors. An entropy
probe uses all 355 training questions, with 123 positives defined by independent normalized-answer
entropy above 0.5 nats. With three independent samples, entropy is −P

Controls include a character 2–4-gram TF-IDF answer-text classifier, answer length, mean negative
