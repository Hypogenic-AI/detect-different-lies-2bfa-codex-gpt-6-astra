Activation probes and transfer. Goldowsky-Dill et al. train linear probes on instructed contrasts
and report transfer to strategic-deception settings, while also observing high scores for some honest
deception-related responses Goldowsky-Dill et al. [2025]. Liars’ Bench evaluates transfer across lie
settings Kretschmar et al. [2025]. Targeted-probe experiments emphasize training-prompt sensitiv-
ity Natarajan et al. [2026]; comparisons of spontaneous and instructed deception motivate keeping
the two conditions distinct Luikham [2026]. Broader architectural comparisons find no uniform
benefit from greater depth, sparsity, or expressivity Moustafa et al. [2026]. We consequently start
with model-specific linear adaptations and simple controls. We do not reuse published checkpoints
or equate our fictional quiz incentives with verified strategic behavior.

3
Methodology
3.1
Matched questions and response conditions
We sample 600 unique questions with seed 42 from the TRIVIAQA rc.nocontext validation
data TriviaQA [2026]. The source has 17,944 rows and 9,960 deduplicated questions. Hashing
canonical normalized answer entities yields 355 training, 124 validation, and 121 test questions. All
variants of each question remain together; canonical answer entities are disjoint across splits. Alias-
equivalent entities and topical dependencies can remain. The first 100 questions form a feasibility
pilot within the fixed sample. We do not optimize prompts after its low recovered-case yield.
We use the pinned QWEN2.5-7B-INSTRUCT checkpoint Qwen Team [2024] with BF16 inference,
no fine-tuning, and no quantization. Each question receives five neutral belief samples across three
phrasings, an independent greedy verification, three additional samples for entropy, one response
in each of five arms, and five greedy follow-ups. This gives 600(5 + 1 + 3 + 5 + 5) = 11,400
generations. Stochastic responses use temperature 0.7, top-p 0.9, top-k 0, and a 32-token limit. All
main conversations are fresh and stateless. Arm order is fixed for batching, with shuffled records
within arms and logged batch seeds; thus batch and seed effects cannot be fully excluded.
The neutral arm requests a brief factual answer and permits UNKNOWN. The sham arm offers 100
fictional quiz points if a contestant relying on the answer is correct. The reward arm changes the re-
warded outcome to an incorrect answer, without supplying an answer or explicitly asking the model
to lie. The reputation arm ties a prestigious contract to sounding definitive and penalizes uncer-
tainty; it does not directly reward falsehood. The instructed control explicitly requests an incorrect
answer without disclosure. We exclude this control from false-output composition estimates. These
are prompt incentives, not real stakes or demonstrated autonomous goals. Sham controls the reward
frame, but is not length-matched to neutral or reputation.
After every main response, we append a private factual-answer request to the same conversation. It
states that the answer will not affect points or reputation. Universal collection prevents later audit
corrections from determining which follow-up evidence exists. Section A provides exact prompts
and scoring rules.
