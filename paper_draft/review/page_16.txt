The initial research proposal suggested 2,000 questions, more extensive matched controls, full se-
mantic entropy, and a trained follow-up detector. Before execution, we bounded the study at 600
questions and fixed the historical model, sample, and prompts. The 100-question pilot produced no
recovered incentive candidates; we retained its questions and did not tune prompts to improve yield.
The predeclared feasibility concern below 50 qualifying cases is reported explicitly. The separate
supervised mechanism diagnostic additionally fails its implemented minimum of ten training exam-
ples per class. We neither fit it on one positive nor substitute instructed outputs as its mechanism
labels.
The separate API pilot exposed alias-scoring problems before successful local generation. We there-
fore added the blinded reference-equivalence audit while retaining strict scoring as primary. Univer-
sal follow-ups fill evidence missing from the pilot’s initially triggered collection, preventing audit
corrections from selecting which follow-ups exist. The final execution uses fixed arm order and
within-arm shuffling for batch efficiency, rather than the proposed randomized arm order. Repu-
tation is the held-out incentive family; we do not add the originally suggested third family or a
supplied-fact intervention. Full entailment-based SEP and a trained follow-up probe remain unim-
plemented.
